**And the evidence for the NEUTRAL conjecture is far weaker than "never
observed" suggests.** Theorem N1 shows a level is non-COLLAPSING **iff** its
predecessor coordinate is eventually zero. There are exactly **four** such
coordinates for `K <= 30000` (`2, 7, 28, 399`), so there were exactly **four
opportunities** for NEUTRAL, and all four resolved as DOUBLING because a parity
came out odd. Four coin-flips, not a mechanism.

## 3. The honest summary

The collapse structure is now completely understood **at the level of the
cycle**. Every level in `[401, 30000]` is COLLAPSING — a chain of 29,600
consecutive collapses — and along it the entire cycle region of the tower is
generated by a **32-bit** transducer.

**And it constrains the diagonal not at all.** The diagonal lives strictly in
the transient (Phase 2C: `T(K) ≈ 1.34K > K`), and `DIAGONAL_DEPENDENCY.md`
measures its backward cone as entirely transient for the last ≈19% of its
history. Registry item **C2** is the sharpest way to see this: its hypothesis is
*verified* on the whole computed range, and its conclusion still does not
follow.

Phase 2D therefore **sharpened rather than crossed** the gap of Phase 1 §10.3:
an argument that never touches the seed-carrying transient cannot settle
Problem 1.

## 5. Controls applied

* Every symbolic identity checked **exhaustively at small sizes** and then
  against the real orbit (2995 levels; 0 mismatches in all four collapse
  checks; ANF re-derived under the seed with 0 mismatches).
* **All-`K` theorems** (R1–R3, CH1–CH3, N1, N2, DD1, DD3) are separated
  throughout from **bounded observations** (`K <= 3000` / `K <= 30000`,
  `t <= 2400`, `t <= 13`).
* **Absence of NEUTRAL up to a finite `K` is not claimed to prove exclusion** —
  §1 restates it as four opportunities with odd parity, and §5 of
  `NEUTRAL_EXCLUSION.md` gives explicit relaxed-model NEUTRAL counterexamples
  showing no seed-free exclusion theorem can exist.
* Failed conjectures preserved: C1 (evidence points against), C2 (hypothesis
  verified, conclusion still does not follow), C3 (unmoved), the variable
  elimination guess (refuted), and the search for a recursion among doubling
  points (nothing found, nothing asserted).
* No formula is fitted to the four doubling points.
